Privacy

Updated: 8 November 2026

Quiera runs on your PC. No account, no advertising, no telemetry: no usage statistics are sent, and nothing goes through a Quiera server.

What goes over the Internet

  • Synced lyrics. To find the lyrics of the playing track, Quiera sends its title, artist, album and duration to LRCLIB, an open lyrics database. NetEase's word-by-word lyrics are an option, off by default; on, the same search also goes to NetEase. To turn off: Settings › Music › Synced lyrics.
  • Updates. At start, then every 6 hours, Quiera reads the list of releases published on GitHub. No personal data is sent; as with any web page, GitHub sees the request's IP address (GitHub's policy). To turn off: Settings › About › Check automatically.
  • Your calendar, if you add it. When you paste a calendar's iCal link (Google, Outlook, iCloud…), Quiera downloads it directly from that address, then every 15 minutes. The link stays on your PC, in the settings, and is never written in the log; events are kept in memory. To turn off: Settings › Calendar, remove the link.
  • What you ask for. A web search from the launcher opens in your browser; so does a link from a notification or an agent.

What stays on your PC

  • Notifications and clipboard history: kept in memory only, erased when Quiera closes.
  • The Shelf: your files' paths (never copied), saved only if you keep the Shelf between sessions.
  • The launcher's file index: file names, in memory.
  • The mirror: the webcam picture shows in the island only while the tab is open. It is neither recorded nor sent.
  • Bluetooth devices' batteries: read from Windows, shown, never sent.
  • Settings, in Quiera's folder (%APPDATA%\app.quiera).
  • The log (%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.quiera\logs): technical events, never a track's title, a notification's content, what you copy or what your AI agents do.

AI agents (Claude Code, Codex)

These features stay off until you turn them on in Settings › AI agents.

  • Configuration. Quiera adds “hooks” to ~/.claude/settings.json or ~/.codex/hooks.json, after a backup copy (.quiera-backup), and a status line to Claude Code if you have none. Turning them off removes these additions without touching your settings.
  • What the island receives. At each step, the agent passes to Quiera, on your PC only: the project folder, the tool used, the file, page or command concerned, and, for a request, the changed code, the plan or the question. Never your messages to the agent. This stays in memory, shows in the island and is gone when Quiera closes.
  • Usage. Usage percentages are read from Claude Code's status line and from Codex's session files (~/.codex/sessions), of which Quiera keeps only the numbers. The latest are saved in agents-usage.json, in Quiera's folder.
  • Your phone. If you show the QR code, your PC serves a page on your local network, at a secret address valid for 8 hours: it shows pending requests. Nothing goes over the Internet. “Unlink the phone” turns it off.
  • Claude Code and Codex talk to Anthropic and OpenAI themselves, under their own rules; Quiera adds nothing to that.

Send to phone

When you ask, files go straight from the PC to the phone over your local network, at a link that only works for 10 minutes. Nothing goes over the Internet or through a Quiera server.

The quiera.eu website

The website is hosted by Vercel, which processes visitors' IP addresses to serve and protect the pages. Visits are counted by Vercel Web Analytics, without cookies: only overall statistics (page views, country, browser) are kept, with nothing that recognises you from one day to the next. The website sets no cookies; only your theme choice is remembered, in your browser.

Your rights

The publisher holds no personal data about you. For any question, open an issue on GitHub. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
